Qualitative content analysis with large language models

Demonstrator for the paper "An open workflow for Mayring qualitative content analysis of nursing interviews with large language models and artificial intelligence agents" (Zöllner, Mages & Maier). Assigns an interview excerpt (nursing: retention, return to practice, recruitment) to one of 27 main categories of Mayring qualitative content analysis. Input may be in any language; the category system itself is German.

In "Whole interview" mode each paragraph (or line) is classified as a separate passage; the result can be downloaded as a CSV file (for Excel). Max. 20 passages per run.

Notes

  • Research demo. Single query at low temperature; the figures in the paper are based on a 5-seed majority vote (temp 0.7) — a single answer may differ.
  • Data processing & privacy. The text you enter is sent to NVIDIA (NIM API) and processed on servers in the USA. It is not stored here, but it leaves the EU. Please do not enter personal or otherwise sensitive data, and check your institution's AI / data-protection policies first. For production use with real interview data we recommend forking the project and using an institution-internal LLM endpoint (switchable via one configuration variable).
  • On the server only the 27 main categories and their definitions are used — no interview passages from the study.
  • Category names and definitions are German (the nursing Mayring system); results are shown with their German labels.

Zöllner, Mages & Maier — LLM-assisted Mayring analysis of German nursing interviews. Code & data: github.com/akmaier/German-nursing-interviews.
